Our Story

Founded in 1964, Tema Technical Institute began with a simple vision: to provide quality technical education that prepares students for real-world success. What started as a small Technical school has grown into one of Ghana's premier technical institutions.

Over the past six decades, we have trained over 5,000 skilled professionals who have gone on to make significant contributions to industries across Africa and beyond. Our alumni include master electricians, mechanical engineers, welding experts, and entrepreneurs who have established their own successful businesses.

Our Mission

To provide accessible, high-quality technical education that equips students with the skills, knowledge, and confidence needed to excel in their chosen fields and contribute to the development of their communities.

Our Vision

To be the leading technical institution in West Africa, recognized for excellence in training, innovation in teaching methods, and producing graduates who are sought after by employers across the continent.

Our Values

  • Excellence: We strive for the highest standards in everything we do.
  • Practical Skills: We believe in learning by doing, with hands-on training at the core of our programs.
  • Innovation: We embrace new technologies and teaching methods to stay ahead of industry trends.
  • Integrity: We operate with honesty, transparency, and respect for all.
  • Community: We foster a supportive learning environment where every student can thrive.

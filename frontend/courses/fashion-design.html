Course Overview

The Fashion Design Technology program provides comprehensive training in all aspects of fashion design and garment construction. From concept development to finished garments, students learn pattern making, draping, sewing techniques, fashion illustration, and textile science. The curriculum combines traditional craftsmanship with modern technology, preparing graduates for careers in the fashion industry.

Students develop their creative skills alongside technical competence, learning to design and create both traditional and contemporary garments. The program emphasizes hands-on practice, with students completing multiple garments and building a professional portfolio. Graduates are prepared for roles in fashion design, garment manufacturing, tailoring, and entrepreneurship.

What You Will Learn

  • Introduction to Fashion: Fashion history, design principles, fashion terminology, and overview of the fashion industry.
  • Sewing Techniques: Machine operation (industrial and domestic), hand stitching, seam finishes, and professional construction techniques.
  • Pattern Making: Drafting basic blocks, pattern manipulation, grading, and creating patterns for different garment types.
  • Draping: Working with muslin on dress forms to create designs directly in fabric.
  • Garment Construction: Step-by-step construction of skirts, blouses, dresses, shirts, trousers, jackets, and traditional wear.
  • Fashion Illustration: Drawing fashion figures, rendering fabrics, technical flats, and presentation boards.
  • Textile Science: Fabric identification, fiber properties, weave structures, fabric performance, and textile testing.
  • Fabric Sourcing and Selection: Understanding fabric qualities, selecting appropriate fabrics for designs, and working with different materials.
  • Fitting and Alterations: Conducting fittings, making adjustments, and alteration techniques.
  • Tailoring Techniques: Advanced construction methods for tailored garments including jackets and coats.
  • Traditional African Fashion: Designing and constructing traditional African garments (Kente, Batakari, Kaba, etc.).
  • Fashion Business: Fashion marketing, branding, pricing, and starting a fashion business.
  • Computer-Aided Design (CAD): Introduction to fashion design software for pattern making and illustration.
  • Portfolio Development: Creating a professional portfolio showcasing design work and completed garments.
  • Fashion Shows and Presentations: Planning and participating in fashion shows and presentations.

Practical Training

Students receive extensive hands-on practice in our fashion studio:

  • Industrial sewing machine operation and maintenance
  • Pattern drafting and grading exercises
  • Draping practice on dress forms
  • Construction of sample garments
  • Fitting sessions with fit models
  • Fabric testing and identification
  • Fashion illustration and portfolio development
  • Final project: Design and construct a complete collection (3-5 garments)

Entry Requirements

  • Basic Education Certificate Examination (BECE) with minimum aggregate as set by the Ghana Education Service
  • Good passes in English, Mathematics, and Integrated Science
  • Interest in fashion and creativity
  • Basic drawing skills (helpful but not required)

Career Opportunities

Graduates of this program can work in various roles including:

  • Fashion Designer: Creating original designs for brands or your own label
  • Pattern Maker: Specializing in creating patterns for garment production
  • Garment Technologist: Working in quality control and production
  • Tailor: Creating custom garments for clients
  • Seamstress: Sewing and alterations services
  • Costume Designer: Creating costumes for theatre, film, and events
  • Fashion Illustrator: Creating fashion illustrations for designers and publications
  • Fashion Stylist: Styling for photoshoots, events, and clients
  • Boutique Owner: Running your own fashion retail business
  • Fashion Buyer: Selecting merchandise for retail stores
  • Production Manager: Overseeing garment production in factories
  • Fashion Educator: Teaching fashion design in institutions

Industries Hiring Fashion Graduates

  • Fashion Houses: Designing and producing clothing lines
  • Garment Manufacturing: Factories producing clothing for local and export markets
  • Retail Companies: Creating private label collections
  • Boutiques: Custom design and alterations
  • Textile Companies: Working with fabric development and quality
  • Theatre and Film: Costume departments
  • Wedding and Event Industry: Bridal and special occasion wear
  • Corporate Uniform Companies: Designing and producing uniforms
  • Fashion Media: Styling for magazines and media

Specializations

Graduates can develop expertise in specialized areas:

  • Bridal Wear: Wedding gowns and bridal party attire
  • African Fashion: Traditional and contemporary African designs
  • Children's Wear: Designing for children
  • Activewear: Sport and performance clothing
  • Tailoring: Men's and women's tailored garments
  • Haute Couture: High-end, custom-designed fashion
  • Sustainable Fashion: Eco-friendly and ethical fashion

Further Studies

After completing this program, students can pursue:

  • Advanced Diploma in Fashion Design
  • Higher National Diploma (HND) in Fashion Design and Textiles
  • Bachelor's degree in Fashion Design or Fashion Merchandising
  • Specialized courses in pattern making, draping, or fashion business
  • International fashion programs and workshops

Modern Fashion Technology

Our program introduces students to modern fashion technology:

  • Industrial sewing machines (single needle, overlock, coverstitch)
  • Digital pattern making software
  • Fashion illustration software
  • Digital fabric printing technology
  • 3D fashion design (introduction)
  • Computerized cutting systems (demonstration)

Course Overview

The Wood Construction Engineering Technology program focuses on all aspects of working with wood, from rough carpentry to fine furniture making. Students learn to use both hand tools and power tools safely and effectively while developing skills in joinery, furniture design, and construction methods. The curriculum covers timber selection, wood properties, joinery techniques, furniture construction, and finishing.

This comprehensive program prepares graduates for careers in carpentry, furniture making, cabinet making, and woodworking. Students develop both traditional craftsmanship skills and modern woodworking techniques, enabling them to work in construction, furniture manufacturing, or to start their own woodworking business.

What You Will Learn

  • Wood Science: Understanding wood properties, timber species, moisture content, wood defects, and timber selection for different applications.
  • Hand Tools: Selection, use, and maintenance of hand tools including saws, planes, chisels, marking tools, and measuring instruments.
  • Power Tools: Safe operation of portable power tools including circular saws, jigsaws, routers, planers, sanders, and drills.
  • Woodworking Machinery: Operation of stationary machinery including table saws, band saws, planers, thicknessers, spindle moulders, and lathes.
  • Joinery Techniques: Various wood joints including butt joints, lap joints, mortise and tenon, dovetail joints, finger joints, and bridle joints.
  • Furniture Making: Design and construction of tables, chairs, cabinets, wardrobes, beds, and other furniture pieces.
  • Construction Carpentry: Framing techniques for walls, floors, and roofs. Installation of doors, windows, and staircases.
  • Cabinet Making: Construction of kitchen cabinets, built-in wardrobes, and storage units including drawer construction and door fitting.
  • Finishing: Sanding techniques, surface preparation, staining, varnishing, lacquering, painting, and waxing.
  • Design and Drawing: Furniture design principles, workshop drawings, cutting lists, and material estimation.
  • Upholstery Basics: Introduction to upholstery techniques for chairs and sofas.
  • Wood Turning: Using wood lathes to create turned components like table legs, spindles, and decorative items.
  • Veneering and Inlay: Application of veneers and decorative inlay techniques.
  • Workshop Safety: Safe working practices, dust extraction, personal protective equipment, and workshop organization.

Practical Training

Students receive extensive hands-on practice in our well-equipped wood workshop:

  • Exercises in using all hand tools and power tools
  • Practice cutting various wood joints
  • Construction of small projects (stools, shelves, small tables)
  • Building full-size furniture pieces (chairs, tables, cabinets)
  • Wood turning exercises on lathes
  • Finishing practice with various techniques
  • Reading and working from workshop drawings
  • Final project: Design and construct a complete furniture piece or wooden structure

Career Opportunities

Graduates of this program can work in various roles including:

  • Carpenter: Working in construction, building houses, installing doors, windows, and roofing
  • Furniture Maker: Designing and building custom furniture pieces
  • Cabinet Maker: Constructing kitchen cabinets, wardrobes, and built-in furniture
  • Woodworker: Creating wooden products, decorative items, and custom pieces
  • Construction Carpenter: Specializing in framing, formwork, and structural carpentry
  • Shop Fitter: Installing shop fittings, displays, and commercial interiors
  • Kitchen Installer: Installing kitchen units and worktops
  • Joinery Workshop Technician: Working in joinery workshops producing doors, windows, and staircases
  • Furniture Restorer: Repairing and restoring antique and modern furniture
  • Self-employed: Starting your own carpentry or furniture making business
  • Woodworking Instructor: Teaching woodworking skills in institutions

Industries Hiring Woodworkers

  • Construction Industry: Building companies, construction sites
  • Furniture Manufacturing: Factories producing furniture
  • Joinery Workshops: Companies producing doors, windows, and staircases
  • Cabinet Making Shops: Custom cabinet and kitchen manufacturers
  • Interior Design Firms: Creating custom interiors and fittings
  • Property Development: Finishing and fitting new properties
  • Hotel and Hospitality: Maintaining and creating furniture for hotels
  • Educational Institutions: Maintaining school furniture and facilities
  • Museums and Heritage Sites: Restoring historic wooden structures and furniture

Specializations

Graduates can develop expertise in specialized areas:

  • Fine Furniture Making: High-end custom furniture
  • Architectural Joinery: Staircases, paneling, custom moldings
  • Musical Instrument Making: Guitars, drums, other instruments
  • Boat Building: Wooden boat construction and repair
  • Wood Carving and Sculpture: Artistic wood creations
  • Timber Frame Construction: Traditional timber frame buildings
  • Conservation and Restoration: Restoring historic wooden artifacts

Further Studies

After completing this program, students can pursue:

  • Advanced Diploma in Wood Technology
  • Higher National Diploma (HND) in Furniture Design and Production
  • Specialized courses in cabinet making, furniture design, or wood finishing
  • Business management courses for starting your own workshop

Modern Woodworking Technology

Our program introduces students to modern woodworking technologies:

  • CNC woodworking machines (introduction)
  • Dust extraction systems
  • Modern adhesives and fasteners
  • Portable power tools and accessories
  • Finishing equipment (spray guns, HVLP systems)
  • Woodworking software for design and cutting lists

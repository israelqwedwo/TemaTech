Course Overview

The Automobile Engineering Technology program provides complete training in automotive repair and maintenance. Students learn to diagnose and repair both petrol and diesel engines, electrical systems, transmissions, braking systems, and modern vehicle electronics. With the automotive industry evolving rapidly, the curriculum includes training on modern diagnostic equipment, engine management systems, and emerging technologies in electric and hybrid vehicles.

This comprehensive program prepares students for careers in the rapidly growing automotive service industry. Graduates will be equipped with the skills needed to work in modern repair shops, dealerships, fleet maintenance facilities, or to start their own automotive repair business.

What You Will Learn

  • Engine Systems: Petrol and diesel engine operation, engine construction, fuel systems (carburetor and fuel injection), cooling systems, lubrication systems, and engine overhaul procedures.
  • Electrical Systems: Automotive batteries, starting systems, charging systems (alternators), ignition systems, lighting, wiring diagrams, and electrical troubleshooting.
  • Transmission Systems: Manual transmissions, clutches, automatic transmissions, driveshafts, differentials, and four-wheel drive systems.
  • Braking Systems: Hydraulic brake systems, disc and drum brakes, brake boosters, anti-lock braking systems (ABS), and electronic brake distribution.
  • Steering and Suspension: Steering systems (rack and pinion, recirculating ball), power steering, suspension components, wheel alignment, and wheel balancing.
  • Vehicle Diagnostics: OBD-II systems, diagnostic scanners, fault code reading, sensor testing, and systematic troubleshooting procedures.
  • Air Conditioning: Automotive AC systems, components, refrigerant handling, leak detection, and AC service procedures.
  • Engine Management: Electronic control units (ECUs), sensors, actuators, fuel injection control, and ignition timing.
  • Hybrid and Electric Vehicles: Introduction to hybrid technology, electric vehicle systems, high-voltage safety, and basic maintenance.

Practical Training

Students work on actual vehicles in our modern automotive workshop, learning to use diagnostic scanners, specialized tools, and repair equipment. Practical experience includes:

  • Complete engine overhaul and reassembly
  • Transmission repair and clutch replacement
  • Brake system service and repair
  • Electrical system diagnostics and wiring repair
  • Suspension and steering system service
  • Air conditioning service and repair
  • Vehicle diagnostic procedures using scan tools
  • Regular service and maintenance procedures (oil changes, tune-ups, inspections)
  • Final project: Complete vehicle diagnosis and repair

Entry Requirements

  • Basic Education Certificate Examination (BECE) with minimum aggregate as set by the Ghana Education Service
  • Good passes in English, Mathematics, and Integrated Science
  • Interest in automobiles and mechanical work
  • Physical fitness for workshop activities

Career Opportunities

Graduates of this program can work in various roles including:

  • Auto Mechanic - Working in repair shops and dealerships
  • Diagnostic Technician - Specializing in vehicle diagnostics and troubleshooting
  • Workshop Supervisor - Managing repair shop operations
  • Service Advisor - Consulting with customers in service centers
  • Fleet Maintenance Technician - Maintaining company vehicle fleets
  • Automotive Electrician - Specializing in vehicle electrical systems
  • Transmission Specialist - Focusing on transmission repair
  • Diesel Mechanic - Working with diesel engines in trucks and equipment
  • Self-employed - Opening your own automotive repair business
  • Mobile Mechanic - Providing on-site repair services

Industry Applications

Automotive technicians are in demand across multiple sectors:

  • Vehicle Dealerships: Authorized service centers for major brands
  • Independent Repair Shops: General automotive repair businesses
  • Fleet Maintenance: Companies with vehicle fleets (delivery services, transport companies)
  • Government Agencies: Maintaining government vehicle fleets
  • Transport Companies: Bus and truck maintenance
  • Emergency Services: Maintaining ambulances, fire trucks, and police vehicles
  • Mining and Construction: Heavy equipment maintenance

Further Studies

After completing this program, students can pursue:

  • Advanced Diploma in Automotive Engineering
  • Higher National Diploma (HND) in Mechanical or Automotive Engineering
  • Manufacturer-specific training (Toyota, Nissan, Ford, etc.)
  • Specialized certifications in diagnostics, transmissions, or air conditioning
  • Hybrid and electric vehicle specialist certification

Modern Automotive Technology

Our program keeps pace with the rapidly evolving automotive industry. Students are introduced to:

  • OBD-II Diagnostics: Modern vehicle diagnostic systems
  • CAN Bus Systems: Vehicle network communication
  • Advanced Driver Assistance Systems (ADAS): Sensors, cameras, and calibration
  • Hybrid Technology: Basic principles and safety procedures
  • Electric Vehicles: Introduction to EV technology

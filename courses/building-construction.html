Course Overview

The Building Construction Engineering Technology program provides comprehensive training in residential and commercial construction methods. From foundation to finishing, students learn all aspects of building construction including materials, techniques, project management, and building codes. The curriculum combines theoretical knowledge with extensive practical skills development.

Students learn to read and interpret architectural drawings, estimate materials and costs, supervise construction projects, and ensure quality control. The program prepares graduates for careers in the construction industry as site supervisors, construction technicians, project managers, or to start their own construction businesses.

What You Will Learn

  • Construction Materials: Properties and applications of concrete, blocks, bricks, timber, steel, reinforcement, and finishing materials. Material testing and quality control.
  • Blueprint Reading: Interpreting architectural drawings, structural drawings, electrical and plumbing plans, and construction specifications.
  • Site Preparation and Surveying: Site clearing, leveling, setting out buildings, use of surveying instruments (theodolite, level, total station).
  • Foundation and Substructure: Soil investigation, foundation types (strip, pad, raft, pile), excavation, footing construction, and foundation walls.
  • Superstructure: Wall construction (blockwork, brickwork), columns, beams, reinforced concrete work, formwork, and scaffolding.
  • Concrete Technology: Concrete mix design, batching, mixing, placing, compaction, curing, and testing. Reinforced concrete principles.
  • Roofing: Roof types (pitched, flat), roof trusses, roof covering materials (tiles, sheets), flashing, and roof insulation.
  • Finishing Works: Plastering, rendering, tiling, painting, ceiling installation, and joinery.
  • Building Services: Integration of electrical wiring, plumbing, drainage, and mechanical systems in buildings.
  • Quantity Surveying: Material take-off, bill of quantities, cost estimation, and tendering processes.
  • Project Management: Construction planning, scheduling, resource management, site organization, and progress monitoring.
  • Building Codes and Regulations: Understanding Ghana building regulations, planning permits, and construction standards.
  • Health and Safety: Construction site safety, risk assessment, personal protective equipment, and safety regulations.
  • Sustainable Construction: Green building practices, energy efficiency, and sustainable materials.

Practical Training

Students participate in extensive practical training including:

  • Setting out buildings using surveying instruments
  • Concrete mixing, placing, and testing
  • Block laying and brickwork exercises
  • Reinforcement bending and fixing
  • Formwork construction
  • Plastering and rendering practice
  • Floor and wall tiling
  • Roof truss assembly and roofing
  • Painting and finishing techniques
  • Construction of small-scale structures on campus
  • Site visits to active construction projects
  • Final project: Complete construction of a model building or small structure

Entry Requirements

  • Basic Education Certificate Examination (BECE) with minimum aggregate as set by the Ghana Education Service
  • Good passes in English, Mathematics, and Integrated Science
  • Physical fitness for construction site work
  • Interest in building and construction

Career Opportunities

Graduates of this program can work in various roles including:

  • Construction Technician: Working on building sites under site managers
  • Site Supervisor: Supervising construction activities and workers
  • Builder: Managing and executing building projects
  • Construction Foreman: Leading construction teams on site
  • Project Assistant: Supporting project managers in construction firms
  • Quantity Surveyor Assistant: Assisting in cost estimation and material take-off
  • Building Inspector: Inspecting construction quality for clients or government
  • Construction Materials Tester: Testing materials in laboratories
  • Self-employed Contractor: Starting your own construction business
  • Real Estate Developer: Developing and managing construction projects

Industries Hiring Construction Technicians

  • Construction Companies: Residential and commercial building contractors
  • Real Estate Developers: Companies developing housing estates and commercial properties
  • Government Agencies: Public works departments, housing authorities
  • Consulting Firms: Construction supervision and project management
  • Mining Companies: Construction and maintenance of facilities
  • NGOs: Construction projects in communities
  • Facilities Management: Maintaining buildings and facilities

Advanced Skills and Specializations

Graduates can develop expertise in specialized areas:

  • Project Management: Advanced construction planning and management
  • Building Information Modeling (BIM): 3D modeling for construction
  • Sustainable Construction: Green building certification (EDGE, LEED)
  • Construction Safety: Specialized safety management
  • Heritage Conservation: Restoration of historic buildings
  • High-rise Construction: Specialized techniques for tall buildings

Further Studies

After completing this program, students can pursue:

  • Advanced Diploma in Building Construction
  • Higher National Diploma (HND) in Building Technology
  • Bachelor's degree in Construction Management or Civil Engineering
  • Professional certifications in project management (PMP, PRINCE2)
  • Quantity Surveying certification

Modern Construction Technology

Our program introduces students to modern construction technologies:

  • Laser levels and digital surveying equipment
  • Concrete testing equipment (slump test, cube testing)
  • Power tools and construction equipment
  • Scaffolding systems and formwork systems
  • Construction management software
  • Building Information Modeling (BIM) basics

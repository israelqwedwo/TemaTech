Course Overview

The Advanced Photography program provides comprehensive training in professional photography techniques and digital editing. Students learn camera operation, lighting, composition, and post-processing skills essential for various photography genres including portrait, event, commercial, and creative photography.

This intensive one-year program combines technical knowledge with artistic vision, preparing graduates for careers as professional photographers. Students work with DSLR and mirrorless cameras, professional lighting equipment, and industry-standard editing software. The curriculum emphasizes hands-on practice, portfolio development, and the business aspects of running a photography business.

What You Will Learn

  • Camera Operation: Understanding camera types (DSLR, mirrorless), settings (aperture, shutter speed, ISO), exposure triangle, and shooting modes.
  • Lenses and Optics: Different lens types (prime, zoom, wide-angle, telephoto), focal length effects, and lens selection for different situations.
  • Composition: Rule of thirds, leading lines, framing, perspective, balance, and visual storytelling techniques.
  • Lighting Fundamentals: Natural light photography, golden hour, understanding light direction and quality.
  • Studio Lighting: Using strobes, continuous lights, modifiers (softboxes, umbrellas, reflectors), and lighting setups for portraits and products.
  • Portrait Photography: Posing techniques, working with subjects, environmental portraits, studio portraits, and headshots.
  • Event Photography: Weddings, parties, corporate events, capturing moments, working quickly, and managing different lighting conditions.
  • Commercial Photography: Product photography, food photography, advertising concepts, and working with clients.
  • Fashion Photography: Working with models, styling, location shoots, and fashion portfolios.
  • Landscape and Travel: Capturing landscapes, cityscapes, travel documentation, and working with natural light.
  • Photo Editing: Adobe Lightroom for cataloging and basic editing, Adobe Photoshop for advanced retouching and manipulation.
  • Color Management: Color correction, color grading, creating mood through color, and consistent output.
  • Portfolio Development: Selecting and presenting your best work, creating a professional portfolio website.
  • Business of Photography: Pricing your work, client communication, contracts, marketing, and social media for photographers.
  • Equipment Knowledge: Tripods, filters, flash systems, and accessories for different photography genres.

Course Overview

The Electrical Engineering Technology program provides students with comprehensive knowledge and practical skills in electrical systems design, installation, and maintenance. Students learn to interpret electrical diagrams, install wiring systems, troubleshoot faults, and maintain electrical equipment.

With a strong emphasis on hands-on training, students work with modern electrical equipment and tools in our well-equipped workshops. The curriculum covers both residential and industrial electrical systems, preparing graduates for diverse career opportunities in the electrical field.

What You Will Learn

  • Introduction to Electrical Principles: Basic electrical theory, Ohm's law, circuit analysis, and electrical measurements.
  • Residential Wiring: House wiring techniques, lighting circuits, socket outlets, distribution boards, and safety regulations.
  • Industrial Electrical Systems: Three-phase systems, electric motors, control panels, industrial safety, and maintenance.
  • Electrical Machine Maintenance: Motor rewinding, transformer maintenance, troubleshooting techniques, and preventive maintenance.
  • Renewable Energy Systems: Solar PV installation, inverters, battery systems, and green energy technologies.
  • Project Work: Complete electrical installation project from design to implementation.

Practical Training

Students gain extensive hands-on experience in our fully equipped electrical workshop, working on real installation projects, fault-finding exercises, and maintenance tasks. Each student completes a final project demonstrating their skills in designing and implementing electrical systems. Our workshop features modern electrical trainers, motor control panels, and testing equipment used in the industry.

Entry Requirements

  • Basic Education Certificate Examination (BECE) with minimum aggregate as set by the Ghana Education Service
  • Good passes in English, Mathematics, and Integrated Science
  • Interest in practical work and technical subjects

Career Opportunities

Graduates of this program can work in various roles including:

  • Licensed Electrician (residential and commercial)
  • Electrical Technician in industries and factories
  • Maintenance Engineer in facilities management
  • Electrical Contractor (self-employed)
  • Solar Installation Technician
  • Facility Manager
  • Electrical Safety Inspector

Further Studies

After completing this program, students can pursue:

  • Advanced Diploma in Electrical Engineering
  • Higher National Diploma (HND) at Polytechnics
  • Bachelor's degree programs at partner universities
  • Professional certifications from electrical bodies
  • Specialized training in renewable energy or industrial automation

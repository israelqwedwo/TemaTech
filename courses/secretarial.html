Course Overview

The Secretarial Option program prepares students for administrative roles in modern offices. Students learn office procedures, document processing, professional communication, and the use of office technology. The curriculum covers both traditional office skills and modern technology applications, preparing graduates for diverse roles in administration and management support.

This comprehensive program develops professional competencies including typing, shorthand, document production, records management, and customer service. Students gain proficiency in Microsoft Office applications and learn to handle multiple tasks efficiently in a professional environment. Graduates are prepared for roles as secretaries, administrative assistants, office managers, and executive assistants.

What You Will Learn

  • Typing and Keyboard Skills: Touch typing techniques, speed development, accuracy, and keyboard proficiency (target: 50+ words per minute).
  • Document Processing: Creating professional letters, memos, reports, and other business documents using Microsoft Word.
  • Shorthand: Learning shorthand systems for rapid note-taking and transcription (optional module).
  • Office Procedures: Reception duties, telephone etiquette, mail handling, scheduling, and office protocols.
  • Records Management: Filing systems (alphabetic, numeric, subject), electronic records management, and archiving.
  • Communication Skills: Business writing, professional correspondence, report writing, and interpersonal communication.
  • Office Technology: Using computers, printers, scanners, photocopiers, and other office equipment.
  • Microsoft Office Suite: Advanced Word, Excel (spreadsheets, basic formulas), PowerPoint (presentations), and Outlook (email and calendar management).
  • Event Coordination: Planning meetings, conferences, and events. Preparing agendas and taking minutes.
  • Travel Arrangements: Booking flights, accommodation, preparing itineraries, and travel documentation.
  • Customer Service: Dealing with visitors, handling inquiries, managing complaints, and building positive relationships.
  • Time Management: Prioritizing tasks, managing multiple deadlines, and organizing work efficiently.
  • Financial Administration: Basic bookkeeping, petty cash management, invoice processing, and expense tracking.
  • Human Resources Support: Maintaining personnel files, assisting with recruitment, and supporting HR processes.
  • Professional Ethics: Confidentiality, discretion, professional conduct, and ethical behavior in the workplace.
  • Business English: Grammar, punctuation, spelling, and professional language usage.

Practical Training

Students receive hands-on training in our modern office simulation lab:

  • Typing practice and speed building exercises
  • Document production from handwritten notes and dictation
  • Creating professional correspondence and reports
  • Filing and records management exercises
  • Reception and telephone handling simulations
  • Meeting organization and minute taking practice
  • Using office equipment (printers, scanners, photocopiers)
  • Computer-based exercises in Word, Excel, PowerPoint, and Outlook
  • Final project: Complete office administration simulation

Career Opportunities

Graduates of this program can work in various roles including:

  • Secretary: Providing administrative support in offices
  • Administrative Assistant: Supporting managers and teams
  • Office Manager: Overseeing office operations and staff
  • Receptionist: Managing front desk and visitor relations
  • Executive Assistant: Supporting senior executives
  • Data Entry Clerk: Processing information in databases
  • Personal Assistant: Providing dedicated support to individuals
  • Customer Service Representative: Handling customer inquiries
  • Records Clerk: Managing files and documents
  • Administrative Officer: Working in government or corporate administration
  • Virtual Assistant: Providing remote administrative support

Industries Hiring Secretarial Graduates

  • Corporate Organizations: All departments need administrative support
  • Government Agencies: Ministries, departments, and agencies
  • Banks and Financial Institutions: Branch and head office administration
  • Educational Institutions: Schools, colleges, and universities
  • Hospitals and Healthcare: Medical administration
  • Hotels and Hospitality: Front office and administration
  • Law Firms: Legal secretarial roles
  • Non-Profit Organizations: Administrative positions
  • Small and Medium Enterprises: Multi-purpose administrative roles
  • International Organizations: NGOs and diplomatic missions

Specializations

Graduates can develop expertise in specialized areas:

  • Legal Secretarial: Working in law firms with legal terminology
  • Medical Secretarial: Healthcare administration and terminology
  • Bilingual Secretary: Working in international organizations
  • Executive Assistant: Supporting C-level executives
  • Office Management: Supervising administrative teams
  • Virtual Assistance: Remote administrative services

Further Studies

After completing this program, students can pursue:

  • Advanced Diploma in Secretarial Studies
  • Higher National Diploma (HND) in Secretaryship and Management Studies
  • Bachelor's degree in Business Administration or Management
  • Professional certifications (CIPS, ICPS)
  • Specialized courses in office management or executive assistance

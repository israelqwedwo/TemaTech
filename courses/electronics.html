Course Overview

The Electronics Engineering Technology program provides comprehensive training in electronic principles, circuit design, and device repair. Students learn to work with analog and digital circuits, microcontrollers, and modern electronic systems used in consumer electronics, communications, and industrial control.

With a strong emphasis on hands-on training, students work in our modern electronics laboratory equipped with oscilloscopes, signal generators, spectrum analyzers, and soldering stations. The curriculum prepares graduates for careers in electronics repair, installation, and maintenance.

What You Will Learn

  • Basic Electronics: Electronic components, soldering techniques, PCB design and fabrication, and circuit assembly.
  • Analog Circuits: Amplifiers, oscillators, power supplies, filters, and signal processing circuits.
  • Digital Electronics: Logic gates, flip-flops, counters, registers, and digital system design.
  • Microcontrollers: Arduino programming, embedded systems, sensors, and interfacing.
  • Communication Systems: Radio frequency circuits, antennas, modulation techniques, and wireless communication.
  • Consumer Electronics: TV, radio, audio equipment repair, and troubleshooting techniques.
  • Industrial Electronics: Motor controls, PLCs, sensors, and industrial automation.

Practical Training

Students spend significant time in our electronics laboratory, learning to use oscilloscopes, signal generators, frequency counters, and other test equipment. Practical exercises include circuit design, PCB fabrication, microcontroller programming, and repair of actual electronic devices. Each student completes a final project such as building a power supply, audio amplifier, or microcontroller-based system.

Entry Requirements

  • Basic Education Certificate Examination (BECE) with minimum aggregate as set by the Ghana Education Service
  • Good passes in English, Mathematics, and Integrated Science
  • Interest in electronics and hands-on work

Career Opportunities

Graduates of this program can work in various roles including:

  • Electronics Technician (TV, radio, audio equipment repair)
  • Consumer Electronics Repair Specialist
  • Instrumentation Officer in industries
  • Telecommunications Technician
  • Solar Inverter Technician
  • Medical Equipment Repairer
  • Self-employed Electronics Repair Business Owner

Further Studies

After completing this program, students can pursue:

  • Advanced Diploma in Electronics Engineering
  • Higher National Diploma (HND) in Electrical/Electronic Engineering
  • Bachelor's degree in Electronics or Telecommunications
  • Specialized certifications in microcontroller programming or industrial electronics

Course Overview

The Mechanical Engineering Technology program provides comprehensive training in mechanical systems, from basic workshop skills to advanced machine operations. Students learn to operate lathes, milling machines, grinders, and other industrial equipment while developing skills in fabrication, maintenance, and mechanical design.

With a strong emphasis on practical skills development, students spend significant time in our well-equipped mechanical workshop working on real projects. The curriculum prepares graduates for careers in manufacturing, maintenance, fabrication, and mechanical systems installation.

What You Will Learn

  • Workshop Practice: Safe use of hand tools, measuring instruments, workshop safety, and basic fabrication techniques.
  • Machine Operations: Lathe work (turning, threading, knurling), milling machine operations, grinding, and drilling.
  • Mechanical Drawing: Blueprint reading, technical drawing, geometric dimensioning, and basic CAD (Computer-Aided Design).
  • Fabrication Techniques: Cutting, bending, forming, welding, and assembly of mechanical components.
  • Mechanical Systems: Power transmission (belts, chains, gears), bearings, lubrication systems, and couplings.
  • Maintenance Practice: Preventive maintenance, troubleshooting techniques, repair procedures, and condition monitoring.
  • Pneumatics and Hydraulics: Fluid power systems, components, circuits, and applications in industrial machinery.

Practical Training

Students receive extensive hands-on training in our mechanical workshop equipped with lathes, milling machines, grinders, drill presses, and fabrication equipment. Practical exercises include:

  • Machining components to precise specifications using lathes and milling machines
  • Fabrication projects including frames, brackets, and mechanical assemblies
  • Assembly and disassembly of mechanical systems
  • Troubleshooting and repair of mechanical equipment
  • Reading and interpreting engineering drawings
  • Final project: Design and fabricate a complete mechanical assembly

Entry Requirements

  • Basic Education Certificate Examination (BECE) with minimum aggregate as set by the Ghana Education Service
  • Good passes in English, Mathematics, and Integrated Science
  • Interest in mechanical work and hands-on activities
  • Physical fitness for workshop activities

Career Opportunities

Graduates of this program can work in various roles including:

  • Mechanical Technician in manufacturing industries
  • Machine Operator in fabrication workshops
  • Maintenance Technician in factories and plants
  • Fabrication Specialist in metal workshops
  • Workshop Supervisor
  • Quality Control Inspector
  • Self-employed in mechanical workshop business
  • Maintenance personnel in hotels, hospitals, and facilities

Further Studies

After completing this program, students can pursue:

  • Advanced Diploma in Mechanical Engineering
  • Higher National Diploma (HND) in Mechanical Engineering
  • Bachelor's degree in Mechanical Engineering or related fields
  • Specialized certifications in CNC machining, CAD/CAM, or industrial maintenance

Industry Applications

Mechanical Engineering Technicians are in demand across multiple sectors:

  • Manufacturing: Production lines, machine operation, quality control
  • Construction: Heavy equipment operation, maintenance
  • Mining: Equipment maintenance, repair workshops
  • Transportation: Vehicle maintenance, fleet management
  • Energy: Power plant maintenance, renewable energy installations
  • Facilities Management: Building systems maintenance

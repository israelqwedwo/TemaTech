Course Overview

The Industrial Mechanics Engineering Technology program prepares students for careers in industrial maintenance and machinery operation. This specialized field focuses on the installation, maintenance, and repair of industrial equipment used in manufacturing plants, processing facilities, factories, and production lines.

Students gain comprehensive knowledge of mechanical systems, fluid power (hydraulics and pneumatics), power transmission, and industrial safety. The curriculum emphasizes practical troubleshooting skills and preventive maintenance procedures essential for keeping industrial operations running smoothly and efficiently.

What You Will Learn

  • Industrial Safety: OSHA standards, hazard identification, lockout/tagout procedures, personal protective equipment, and safety regulations in industrial environments.
  • Hydraulic Systems: Hydraulic principles, pumps, valves, actuators, cylinders, hydraulic circuits, troubleshooting, and maintenance of hydraulic systems.
  • Pneumatic Systems: Compressed air systems, pneumatic components, air preparation units, actuators, control valves, and pneumatic circuit design.
  • Power Transmission: Bearings (ball, roller, plain), gears (spur, helical, bevel, worm), belts (V-belts, timing belts), chains, couplings, clutches, and brakes.
  • Pumps and Compressors: Centrifugal pumps, positive displacement pumps, reciprocating compressors, rotary compressors, installation, maintenance, and troubleshooting.
  • Conveyor Systems: Belt conveyors, roller conveyors, chain conveyors, maintenance, tracking, and troubleshooting.
  • Preventive Maintenance: Maintenance scheduling, lubrication programs, condition monitoring, vibration analysis, thermal imaging, and predictive maintenance techniques.
  • Mechanical Seals and Packing: Types of mechanical seals, installation, troubleshooting, and gland packing for pumps and valves.

Practical Training

Students receive extensive hands-on training in our industrial mechanics laboratory, which features working industrial equipment including:

  • Hydraulic and pneumatic training benches with industrial components
  • Various pump types (centrifugal, gear, diaphragm) for hands-on maintenance practice
  • Air compressor systems for pneumatic training
  • Conveyor systems for alignment and tracking exercises
  • Gearbox assemblies for disassembly and inspection
  • Bearing fitting and alignment exercises
  • Real-world troubleshooting scenarios and fault-finding exercises
  • Final project: Complete maintenance plan for an industrial system

Career Opportunities

Graduates of this program are in high demand across various industries:

  • Industrial Mechanic - Maintaining and repairing machinery in factories and plants
  • Maintenance Technician - Performing preventive and corrective maintenance in manufacturing facilities
  • Plant Maintenance Engineer - Overseeing maintenance operations in processing plants
  • Hydraulic Specialist - Working with hydraulic systems in heavy industries
  • Pneumatic Systems Technician - Maintaining compressed air systems and pneumatic controls
  • Conveyor Systems Technician - Installing and maintaining material handling systems
  • Facility Maintenance Supervisor - Managing maintenance teams in large facilities
  • Mining Maintenance Technician - Working in the mining industry maintaining heavy equipment
  • Manufacturing Technician - Supporting production lines in factories

Industries Hiring Graduates

  • Manufacturing: Food processing, beverage production, consumer goods, automotive assembly
  • Mining: Mineral processing plants, heavy equipment maintenance
  • Oil and Gas: Refineries, pipelines, storage facilities
  • Power Generation: Thermal plants, hydroelectric facilities, renewable energy
  • Agriculture: Processing facilities, grain handling, irrigation systems
  • Construction: Heavy equipment maintenance, construction machinery
  • Ports and Logistics: Cargo handling equipment, conveyor systems

Further Studies

After completing this program, students can pursue:

  • Advanced Diploma in Industrial Maintenance Engineering
  • Higher National Diploma (HND) in Mechanical Engineering
  • Specialized certifications in hydraulics, pneumatics, or vibration analysis
  • Bachelor's degree in Mechanical or Industrial Engineering
  • Certified Maintenance and Reliability Professional (CMRP)

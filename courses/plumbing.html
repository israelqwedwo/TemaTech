Course Overview

The Plumbing and Gas Fitting Engineering Technology program prepares students for careers in residential and commercial plumbing, as well as gas installation and fitting. The curriculum covers water supply systems, drainage, sanitation, and gas pipe installation with a strong emphasis on safety, building codes, and industry standards.

Students learn to read plumbing drawings, select appropriate materials, and install complete plumbing systems for buildings. Gas fitting training includes the installation and maintenance of gas pipes, appliances, and safety devices. This program meets the requirements for licensing as a plumber and gas fitter in Ghana.

What You Will Learn

  • Plumbing Materials and Tools: Types of pipes (PVC, copper, galvanized, PEX), fittings, valves, fixtures, and specialized plumbing tools and equipment.
  • Water Supply Systems: Cold and hot water distribution systems, water pressure, pipe sizing, pumping systems, and water storage tanks.
  • Drainage Systems: Soil, waste, and vent (SWV) systems, drainage pipe installation, gradients, traps, and cleanouts.
  • Sanitary Fixtures: Installation of toilets, sinks, urinals, showers, bathtubs, and bidets according to manufacturer specifications and building codes.
  • Gas Fitting: Gas pipe materials and sizing, gas appliance installation, pressure testing, leak detection, and safety devices.
  • Plumbing Design and Blueprint Reading: Interpreting architectural and plumbing drawings, isometric drawings, and system design principles.
  • Roof and Storm Water Drainage: Gutters, downpipes, roof drains, and storm water management systems.
  • Pumps and Pumping Systems: Booster pumps, sump pumps, sewage pumps, installation, and maintenance.
  • Water Heaters: Installation of electric, gas, and solar water heaters, including safety controls and temperature regulation.
  • Plumbing Codes and Standards: Understanding Ghana plumbing codes, building regulations, and industry standards.
  • Health and Safety: Safe work practices, personal protective equipment, hazard identification, and working in confined spaces.
  • Customer Service: Communicating with customers, providing quotations, and managing plumbing projects.

Practical Training

Hands-on training in our plumbing workshop with real installations and mock-up bathrooms:

  • Pipe cutting, threading, bending, and jointing for various pipe materials
  • Installation of complete bathroom suites including toilets, sinks, and showers
  • Assembly of water supply and drainage systems
  • Gas pipe installation and pressure testing
  • Fabrication of pipe supports and brackets
  • Installation of water heaters and pumps
  • Leak detection and repair exercises
  • Reading and working from plumbing drawings
  • Final project: Complete plumbing system design and installation for a model house

Entry Requirements

  • Basic Education Certificate Examination (BECE) with minimum aggregate as set by the Ghana Education Service
  • Good passes in English, Mathematics, and Integrated Science
  • Physical fitness and ability to work in confined spaces
  • Good problem-solving skills

Career Opportunities

Graduates of this program can work in various roles including:

  • Plumber: Working in residential, commercial, and industrial settings
  • Pipe Fitter: Installing and maintaining piping systems in industrial facilities
  • Gas Fitter: Installing and maintaining gas systems and appliances
  • Plumbing Contractor: Starting your own plumbing business
  • Maintenance Plumber: Working for facilities management companies
  • Construction Plumber: Working on new building construction projects
  • Sewage and Drainage Technician: Working on municipal drainage systems
  • Plumbing Inspector: Inspecting plumbing installations for compliance
  • Water and Sanitation Technician: Working on water and sanitation projects

Industries Hiring Plumbers

  • Construction Industry: Residential and commercial building projects
  • Facilities Management: Maintaining plumbing systems in hotels, hospitals, schools, and offices
  • Municipal Water and Sewage: Working for water and sanitation companies
  • Gas Companies: Installing and maintaining gas systems
  • Manufacturing: Industrial plumbing and pipe fitting
  • Mining: Industrial plumbing and pipe systems
  • Oil and Gas: Specialized pipe fitting (with additional training)

Licensing and Certification

Graduates can pursue professional licensing:

  • Plumbing Trade Test Certification
  • Gas Fitter License from the Energy Commission
  • Plumbing Contractor License
  • City & Guilds Plumbing Certification
  • NVQ in Plumbing and Gas Fitting

Specialized Skills

Advanced areas students can specialize in:

  • Medical Gas Systems: Installation of medical gas systems in hospitals
  • Fire Sprinkler Systems: Installation of fire suppression systems
  • Solar Water Heating: Specializing in solar thermal systems
  • Water Treatment: Water purification and treatment systems
  • Rainwater Harvesting: Sustainable water collection systems

Course Overview

The Accounting Option program provides students with foundational knowledge and practical skills in financial accounting, bookkeeping, and business management. Students learn to maintain financial records, prepare accounts, and use computerized accounting systems. The curriculum covers both manual and computerized accounting, preparing students for entry-level positions in finance and administration.

This program is ideal for students who are detail-oriented and interested in working with numbers and financial information. Graduates are prepared for roles in accounting departments, as bookkeepers, accounts clerks, and finance assistants in various organizations. The program also provides a solid foundation for those wishing to pursue professional accounting qualifications.

What You Will Learn

  • Principles of Accounting: Basic accounting concepts, double-entry bookkeeping, the accounting equation, and the accounting cycle.
  • Financial Accounting: Preparation of final accounts (income statement, balance sheet) for sole proprietors, partnerships, and small businesses.
  • Bookkeeping: Recording transactions in journals, posting to ledgers, trial balance preparation, and bank reconciliation.
  • Cash and Bank Transactions: Managing cash books, petty cash, bank accounts, and reconciling bank statements.
  • Accounts Receivable and Payable: Managing customer and supplier accounts, invoicing, credit control, and payment processing.
  • Inventory Accounting: Stock valuation methods, inventory records, and cost of goods sold calculation.
  • Fixed Assets and Depreciation: Recording asset purchases, calculating depreciation, and asset disposal.
  • Payroll Accounting: Calculating wages and salaries, statutory deductions (PAYE, Social Security), and payroll records.
  • Computerized Accounting: Using accounting software (QuickBooks, Sage, Tally) for recording transactions and generating reports.
  • Spreadsheets for Accounting: Using Excel for financial calculations, data analysis, and creating accounting templates.
  • Business Mathematics: Percentages, ratios, interest calculations, discounts, and markups.
  • Cost Accounting: Basic cost concepts, costing methods, and cost analysis for decision making.
  • Taxation Basics: Introduction to direct and indirect taxes, VAT, and basic tax computations.
  • Financial Management: Budgeting, cash flow management, and basic financial analysis.
  • Business Communication: Writing business letters, memos, reports, and professional communication skills.
  • Ethics in Accounting: Professional ethics, confidentiality, and integrity in accounting practice.

Practical Training

Students receive hands-on training in our computer lab and through practical exercises:

  • Manual bookkeeping exercises from source documents to trial balance
  • Preparation of final accounts for sample businesses
  • Bank reconciliation exercises
  • Payroll calculations and record keeping
  • Using accounting software to record transactions and generate reports
  • Excel exercises for accounting applications
  • Case studies and real-world scenarios
  • Final project: Complete accounting cycle for a simulated business

Entry Requirements

  • Basic Education Certificate Examination (BECE) with minimum aggregate as set by the Ghana Education Service
  • Good passes in English, Mathematics, and Integrated Science
  • Attention to detail and accuracy
  • Interest in working with numbers

Career Opportunities

Graduates of this program can work in various roles including:

  • Accounts Clerk: Processing financial transactions in organizations
  • Bookkeeper: Maintaining financial records for small businesses
  • Payroll Clerk: Processing payroll in companies
  • Finance Assistant: Supporting finance departments
  • Accounts Payable/Receivable Clerk: Managing supplier and customer accounts
  • Tax Assistant: Assisting with tax preparation and filing
  • Audit Assistant: Supporting audit teams in accounting firms
  • Bank Teller: Working in banking institutions
  • Administrative Assistant: Combining accounting with general office duties
  • Self-employed: Offering bookkeeping services to small businesses

Industries Hiring Accounting Graduates

  • Accounting Firms: Assisting with client accounts and tax preparation
  • Banks and Financial Institutions: Teller positions, customer service
  • Corporate Organizations: Accounting and finance departments
  • Small and Medium Enterprises: Bookkeeping and accounts roles
  • Government Agencies: Finance and accounts departments
  • Non-Profit Organizations: Finance and administration
  • Retail Companies: Sales and accounts roles
  • Hotels and Hospitality: Front office and accounts
  • Educational Institutions: Finance departments

Professional Qualifications

Graduates can pursue professional accounting certifications:

  • ICAG (Institute of Chartered Accountants, Ghana): Path to becoming a Chartered Accountant
  • ACCA (Association of Chartered Certified Accountants): International accounting qualification
  • CIMA (Chartered Institute of Management Accountants): Management accounting focus
  • ATSWA (Accounting Technicians Scheme, West Africa): Accounting technician qualification
  • CIPFA: Public finance accounting

Further Studies

After completing this program, students can pursue:

  • Advanced Diploma in Accounting
  • Higher National Diploma (HND) in Accountancy
  • Bachelor's degree in Accounting, Finance, or Business Administration
  • Professional accounting qualifications (ICAG, ACCA, CIMA)
  • Specialized courses in taxation, auditing, or financial management

Course Overview

The Hospitality and Catering Management program prepares students for exciting careers in the food service and hospitality industry. Students learn professional cooking techniques, food safety and hygiene, menu planning, catering operations, and hospitality management principles. The curriculum combines theoretical knowledge with extensive practical training in our professional kitchen and simulated restaurant environment.

This comprehensive program covers all aspects of hospitality operations, from kitchen management to customer service, preparing graduates for roles in restaurants, hotels, catering companies, and institutional food service operations. Students develop both culinary skills and management capabilities essential for success in this dynamic industry.

What You Will Learn

  • Food Safety and Hygiene: Personal hygiene, food handling, cross-contamination prevention, HACCP principles, temperature control, and sanitation procedures.
  • Kitchen Organization: Kitchen layout, workflow, equipment identification and use, mise en place, and kitchen brigade system.
  • Basic Cooking Techniques: Knife skills, heat transfer methods (boiling, steaming, frying, roasting, grilling), and foundation sauces.
  • Stock, Soups, and Sauces: Preparation of stocks (chicken, beef, fish, vegetable), various soups, and classic sauce preparations.
  • Vegetable and Starch Cookery: Preparation of vegetables, potatoes, rice, pasta, and other starches.
  • Meat, Poultry, and Fish: Butchery, portioning, cooking methods for different cuts, and fish preparation techniques.
  • Baking and Pastry: Bread making, cakes, pastries, desserts, and basic patisserie techniques.
  • Menu Planning: Menu design, nutrition considerations, costing, and menu engineering.
  • Catering Operations: Quantity food production, buffet setup, event planning, and off-premise catering.
  • Restaurant Service: Front-of-house operations, service styles, customer service, and handling complaints.
  • Beverage Management: Non-alcoholic beverages, coffee and tea service, bar operations (optional module).
  • Kitchen Management: Stock control, ordering, inventory management, and kitchen administration.
  • Cost Control: Food costing, portion control, waste management, and profitability analysis.
  • Customer Service: Communication skills, handling customer expectations, and building customer loyalty.
  • Health and Safety: Kitchen safety, fire safety, first aid, and workplace regulations.

Practical Training

Students receive extensive hands-on training in our professional training kitchen and simulated restaurant:

  • Basic knife skills and vegetable cutting exercises
  • Preparation of stocks, soups, and sauces
  • Cooking practice for various meat, poultry, and fish dishes
  • Baking and pastry production
  • Menu planning and recipe development
  • Quantity cooking for catering events
  • Restaurant service practice (front-of-house)
  • Buffet setup and presentation
  • Catering for actual campus events
  • Final project: Plan and execute a complete catering event

Entry Requirements

  • Basic Education Certificate Examination (BECE) with minimum aggregate as set by the Ghana Education Service
  • Good passes in English, Mathematics, and Integrated Science
  • Interest in food and hospitality
  • Good communication and interpersonal skills
  • Physical fitness for kitchen work

Career Opportunities

Graduates of this program can work in various roles including:

  • Chef: Commis chef, demi-chef, chef de partie in restaurants and hotels
  • Caterer: Running catering businesses for events and functions
  • Restaurant Manager: Managing restaurant operations
  • Hotel Staff: Working in hotel food and beverage departments
  • Food Service Supervisor: Supervising food service in institutions (schools, hospitals, companies)
  • Kitchen Manager: Managing kitchen operations in restaurants or institutions
  • Pastry Chef: Specializing in baking and pastry
  • Banquet Coordinator: Organizing banquets and events
  • Food Stylist: Preparing food for photography and media
  • Self-employed: Starting your own catering business, food truck, or restaurant
  • Hospitality Trainer: Teaching hospitality skills in institutions

Industries Hiring Hospitality Graduates

  • Hotels and Resorts: Restaurants, banquets, room service
  • Restaurants: Fine dining, casual dining, fast food
  • Catering Companies: Event catering, corporate catering
  • Institutional Food Service: Schools, universities, hospitals, corporate cafeterias
  • Cruise Ships: International cruise line kitchens
  • Airlines: Airline catering services
  • Event Management Companies: Wedding and event catering
  • Bakeries and Patisseries: Pastry and baked goods production
  • Food Manufacturing: Test kitchens, product development

Specializations

Graduates can develop expertise in specialized areas:

  • Patisserie: Specializing in desserts, pastries, and baked goods
  • Garde Manger: Cold kitchen, salads, appetizers, and charcuterie
  • International Cuisine: Specializing in specific cuisines (African, European, Asian)
  • Nutrition and Health: Healthy cooking, special diets, nutritional planning
  • Wine and Beverage: Sommelier training, beverage management
  • Event Management: Specializing in wedding and corporate event catering

Further Studies

After completing this program, students can pursue:

  • Advanced Diploma in Hospitality Management
  • Higher National Diploma (HND) in Hospitality Management
  • Bachelor's degree in Hospitality or Tourism Management
  • International certifications (City & Guilds, CTH)
  • Specialized culinary arts programs

International Opportunities

Hospitality qualifications open doors to international careers:

  • Work in hotels and restaurants worldwide
  • International cruise ship employment
  • Resort destinations across Africa and beyond
  • Airline catering and hospitality
  • International event management
